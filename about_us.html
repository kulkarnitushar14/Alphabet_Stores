Our Story

Elevating the everyday. We believe that the objects you interact with daily should bring a sense of peace, order, and beauty to your life.

Studio Photo (Mumbai)

Curating the Essentials.

Alphabet Store was founded on a simple principle: everyday items do not have to be ordinary. Frustrated by the sea of disposable, poorly designed home goods, we set out to build a sanctuary for minimalist aesthetics and high functionality.

What started as a small curated collection of kitchen organizers has blossomed into a full lifestyle brand. Every piece in our catalogue is rigorously tested by our team to ensure it earns its place in your home.

Our Design Philosophy

Form Meets Function

We believe true beauty lies in utility. If a product doesn't solve a problem flawlessly, its aesthetic doesn't matter.

Sustainable Sourcing

Working exclusively with ethical manufacturers. We prioritize materials like bamboo, glass, and recyclable metals over single-use plastics.

Built to Last

We reject fast consumerism. Our goods are crafted for longevity, saving you money and reducing environmental waste over time.
